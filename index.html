Services

Cover we arrange

We work with leading insurers to structure the right cover for each risk, from everyday personal policies to complex corporate and liability programmes.

  • Motor and personal

    Motor insurance, home and contents, travel and personal accident policies.

  • Corporate and commercial

    Property, fire, burglary and business interruption cover, built around how your business operates.

  • Health and life

    Group health schemes, individual medical cover, life assurance and credit life for staff and individuals.

  • Marine and cargo

    Import and export cargo, marine hull and freight liability for businesses in international trade.

  • Liability and professional

    Public liability, professional indemnity, directors and officers, and product liability.

  • Risk consulting

    Independent risk assessments, coverage gap analysis and reviews of your existing policies.

How we work

One broker, from first conversation to claim

  1. Tell us what you need to protect

About MIB

An independent brokerage, accountable to its clients

Multinational Insurance Brokers is an independent brokerage firm. We act solely in the interest of our clients, not the insurers, so our advice is unbiased and our cover is competitively placed.

We advise individuals, SMEs, large corporates and institutions, with a team that knows the insurance market and the regulations that govern it.
