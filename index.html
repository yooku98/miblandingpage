About MIB

Independent Expertise, Global Reach

Multinational Insurance Brokers (MIB) is an independent brokerage firm committed to delivering tailored insurance solutions that protect what matters most. We act solely in the interest of our clients — not the insurers — ensuring unbiased advice and competitive coverage.

From individuals and SMEs to large corporates and institutions, our team brings deep industry knowledge, professional rigour, and a genuine commitment to client outcomes.

Client First We represent you, not the insurer. Independent advice, always.
Professional Rigour Accredited professionals with deep regulatory and market knowledge.

What We Cover

Comprehensive Coverage Solutions

We work with leading insurers to structure the right cover for every risk profile — from everyday personal lines to complex corporate and liability solutions.

Motor & Personal Lines

Comprehensive motor insurance, home and contents cover, travel, and personal accident policies tailored to individual needs.

Corporate & Commercial

Property, fire, burglary, and business interruption cover for businesses of all sizes — structured around your operational risk profile.

Health & Life

Group health schemes, individual medical insurance, life assurance, and credit life products for employees and individuals.

Marine & Cargo

Import and export cargo insurance, marine hull, and freight liability cover for businesses engaged in international trade.

Liability & Professional

Public liability, professional indemnity, directors & officers liability, and product liability policies for professionals and corporates.

Risk Consulting

Independent risk assessments, coverage gap analysis, and policy review services to ensure your portfolio is properly structured.
